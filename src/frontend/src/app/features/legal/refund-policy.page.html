This Refund Policy explains the circumstances under which KnowledgeMarket offers refunds for purchases made through the Service. By making a purchase you agree to this policy.

1. One-Time Course Purchases

We offer a 7-day refund window for one-time course purchases. You may request a refund within seven (7) calendar days of the purchase if you have viewed less than 25% of the lesson content in that course. After seven days, or after you have viewed 25% or more of the course, the purchase is final.

2. Subscriptions

Subscriptions are billed on a recurring basis. You may cancel at any time from your account settings, and your subscription will remain active until the end of the current billing period. We do not provide partial refunds for unused portions of a billing period. Renewals are not refundable once the new billing period has begun, except as required by applicable law.

3. How to Request a Refund

Send an email to noahstarkenburg@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account. Include the order ID and the reason for your request. We typically respond within five (5) business days.

4. Exceptions

We may issue refunds outside the standard window in cases of:

  • Duplicate charges or technical errors that resulted in incorrect billing.
  • A course being removed from the platform before you completed it.
  • Material misrepresentation of course content by the creator.
  • Refunds required by applicable consumer protection law.

5. Chargebacks

Before initiating a chargeback with your bank or card issuer, please contact us so we can resolve the issue. Chargebacks issued without first contacting us may result in suspension of your account.

6. Refund Method

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method. The time it takes for the refund to appear in your account depends on your bank or card issuer and is typically between three (3) and ten (10) business days.

7. Course Creator Payouts

If a refund is issued for a course you sold, the corresponding payout will be reversed or deducted from a future payout.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. The version in effect at the time of your purchase applies to that purchase.
